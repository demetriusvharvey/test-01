# Phase 5: availability and scenarios

The daily NFL shadow job now captures recent nflverse injury/practice rows with the actual observation time, raw source `date_modified`, a source-file hash and attribution. The latter timestamp is preserved as reported, not asserted to be a publication time. The as-of reader rejects future/stale snapshots, leaves missing players unknown, and reports conflicting same-snapshot statuses as unknown. These observations are research-only; no current prediction assumes a starter, player health, or probability of playing.

[nflverse](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/nflverse_data_schedule.html) says the injury dataset updates daily at 7 a.m. UTC; this application's scheduled capture is daily, not real-time. [Its dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_injuries.html) identifies `date_modified`, report status and practice status. The project records CC BY 4.0 attribution and the unresolved rights of underlying data providers. Data captured after a game cannot be backdated into a pregame model.

Shadow helpers estimate a shrunk status-conditioned play rate from **earlier observed reports and earlier confirmed participation**, and mix explicitly supplied joint availability scenarios and player impacts, returning variance. They reject unobserved future information, missing player impacts and incomplete scenario weights. Synthetic tests verify the math and leakage gates; they do not demonstrate predictive accuracy or train a player-impact model. No injury adjustment or post-inactives wager phase is promoted, and the output never permits staking.

**Still missing:** an NBA injury/lineup source with clear betting and redistribution rights, actual publication/inactive timing, confirmed lineup/quarterback snapshots, RAPM/BPM-type player impacts, verified player participation, enough samples for a calibrated status model, cross-player correlations and two-season/holdout comparisons. [NBA terms](https://www.nba.com/termsofuse) restrict NBA Statistics in gambling contexts; how they apply to injury-report reuse must be established before building an automated NBA betting feed. A public link or copied report does not itself grant redistribution permission. Do not infer `healthy` from absence of a report.

For NFL picks, the optional context can now show entries from the observed, matching-week nflverse injury/practice snapshot, with observation time and source attribution. It requires a matching pregame schedule and a report captured within 30 hours. Missing names do not imply health, and the display does not confirm the starting QB or game-day inactive list. It does not change the prediction or assume when a status was first published.
