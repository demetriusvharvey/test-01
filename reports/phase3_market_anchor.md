# Phase 3: market anchor and price diagnostics

Shadow utilities can devig a two-sided market using multiplicative, power or Shin methods; exclude stale, postgame, future, incomplete or invalid observations; distinguish retail, sharp, exchange and prediction-market sources; account for supplied exchange fees; require verified rights, source classification, upstream identity and observable liquidity for non-retail candidates; and avoid counting the same upstream feed twice. Missing sharp data produces an explicitly labeled retail-median fallback. A chronological weight-fitting helper requires prior settled samples and remains unpromoted. Underround detection is informational only, not an order or an executable arbitrage.

**Operational status: no qualified independent sharp feed or certified sharp close is configured.** No method or weighting has won a walk-forward comparison, no cross-source steam, reverse-line movement or executable liquidity has been validated, and no sharp CLV can be calculated. The user-approved model-first amendment keeps the market out of the model's probability and uses price only for paper selection and benchmarking. Nothing here places a bet or authorizes real-money staking.

[The Odds API bookmaker catalog](https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/bookmaker-apis.html) lists Pinnacle in the EU region (its public-site prices may be delayed), Betfair Exchange in the UK/EU regions and several US exchanges. A listing is not proof of the user's plan access, timely quotes, liquidity, usable history, independent origin, or local legal availability. [API guidance](https://the-odds-api.com/liveapi/guides/v4/) charges by region and market; the current optional key is missing, so no extra paid calls or new account were made. Kalshi/Polymarket fees, orderbook depth, product rules and state restrictions need separate source-specific verification before those prices can be used. No recommendation is made to trade on those venues.

Future tests: preregister method/weights and outcome metrics, fit weights only on earlier captured, settled games, compare against equal weights and matched-time retail forecasts, log failures and adjust across the research family. Current sample of qualified sharp matched-time observations: zero.
