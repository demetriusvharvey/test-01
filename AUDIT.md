# Step 0 audit

Audit of `main` at `ba9853b71b33ecf82fd73f06954a1ba0400a6900` (October 27, 2026 ET). This is an engineering and research audit, not proof of an edge. Source permissions and update timing are documented in [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md).

## Verified current state

- `scripts/pipeline.py` is the active, standard-library-only ingestion, model, selection, grading and static-board program. `scripts/refresh_feed.py` separately writes `dist/feed.json`; its FanDuel-only The Odds API data do **not** drive picks. `dist/data.js` exists but is not imported by `dist/index.html`.
- The model combines a median proportional no-vig retail market estimate with Elo at 75:25. It allows up to three single-game picks per day when at least three books meet the price rule. Picks are recorded before game time and graded only on a final score. The record at this audit contains one pending NBA pick, zero wins and zero losses: no meaningful observed accuracy, ROI or CLV is available.
- Contrary to the master prompt's initial description, the current model also evaluates rest and five-game scoring margin. The latest board reports both as active in NBA and NFL after a two-season **team-component** Brier check. The evaluation was exploratory and neither preregistered nor FDR-controlled; its later season has been used repeatedly, so it is not an untouched final holdout. It does not compare the full market blend, closing lines or actual betting returns.
- Five BoxScore Lab seasons are listed, but the newest is in progress. The NFL parser currently discards the other useful `games.csv` fields. NBA referee assignments are archived from the NBA site only when the page date is verified; the archived `observed_at` is first observation, **not** a verified first-publication time. NFL referee effects and injury features are not used.
- `daily-picks.yml` is configured hourly; `refresh-feed.yml` runs twice daily. As of this snapshot, push-triggered runs succeeded, but no schedule-triggered run was found in the recent run history. GitHub schedules can delay or skip executions; freshness and missed runs need explicit monitoring. Twelve existing tests pass.

## Top ten weaknesses, ranked by likely effect on trustworthy profitability claims

1. **No measured edge against market prices.** There is no matched-time market-only benchmark, sharp closing price, CLV, slippage, return, confidence interval or meaningful forward sample. Accuracy alone cannot establish profitable betting.
2. **Feature promotion is not compliant with the research protocol.** Rest/form were selected and enabled on reused historical seasons without preregistration, multiple-testing correction, two replications plus an untouched holdout, or evidence for the full blend. Treat the existing result as exploratory; freeze these additions to shadow mode pending the required validation.
3. **No append-only, point-in-time odds history or reliable closes.** The board keeps a single latest snapshot and initial prices only for selected games. There is no full market history or reliable way to reconstruct a price just before start. A last observed pregame quote is a proxy, not necessarily the true close or a sharp close.
4. **The only active market input is one hourly aggregator.** The six retail prices can be correlated, delayed, wrong or unavailable; the separate FanDuel feed is not a fallback. Bookmakers, timestamps and source agreements need cross-checking.
5. **Training and decision evaluation can leak or overfit.** Season-wide data are updated after games, candidates and parameters are tuned on repeatedly reused periods, and team-only Brier does not test the combined pick procedure. Data release time and data revisions are not fully captured.
6. **Injuries, lineups, starting quarterbacks and scenario uncertainty are absent.** Market prices can reflect news, but a delayed source may not; the model has no direct news freshness check or way to quantify uncertainty.
7. **Selection economics are unvalidated.** The 1-point edge threshold, 75:25 blend and three-pick cap are assumptions. There is no walk-forward selection evaluation at actually available prices, no fees/limits/slippage or bankroll/risk accounting.
8. **Source rights and lineage need resolution.** BoxScore Lab explicitly licenses its published CSVs under CC BY 4.0 but says they are derived from NBA Stats; NBA.com terms restrict gambling use of NBA Statistics. The applicability to a downstream third-party license is unresolved. AgentBets permits public agent lookups, but independent rights to bulk redistribution of page-derived raw data have not been verified. nflverse says underlying data carry their own owners' terms. Direct NBA stats/officials/injury backfills and other sources must not be presumed licensed.
9. **Reliability and data quality are incomplete.** Schedules can delay, HTML can change, team aliases and start times can mismatch, and there is no persistent reconciliation, quota budget, duplicate audit, source failure isolation or test with recorded provider fixtures. Existing stale checks reduce but do not eliminate those risks.
10. **No reproducible research registry.** There is no frozen feature/data snapshot per decision, preregistration, negative-result log, FDR adjustment, model registry, pattern reports or independently untouched confirmation season. Referee observations are sparse and cannot support individual causal claims.

No ROI, CLV, p-values or effectiveness claims are estimated here because the necessary qualified data and protocol do not exist.
