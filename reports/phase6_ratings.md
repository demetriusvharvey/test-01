# Phase 6: team ratings and schedule features

`ratings_v2.py` provides a pure-model **shadow challenger** with margin-scaled Elo updates, team home effects shrunk toward a league prior, preseason rating carryover, and a descriptive rating-scale uncertainty measure. A candidate grid for carryover (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1) can be selected chronologically from pregame forecasts and separately observed prior finals, with at least 500 games across four seasons by default. It never changes the production/paper champion automatically and its rating uncertainty is not a calibrated probability interval. Late prior-season corrections do not roll a current rating backwards.

A separate time-gated schedule helper reports rest, back-to-backs, recent game density, venue-to-venue great-circle distance and time-zone offset changes only where venue and game data were observed before the forecast. Venue distance is not a verified travel itinerary. These features are not active model inputs.

Synthetic tests establish chronology and arithmetic only. **No qualified walk-forward rating comparison has been run**, and H008 remains untested. The current available historical game sources do not provide a complete sequence of captured pregame forecasts/final-observation timestamps; retrospective rows cannot fill that gap. Team-specific calibrated offense/defense, lineup continuity, preseason futures, state-space estimation, QB-adjusted EPA, shot-luck, travel and rotation effects are not complete or validated. The new challenger must pass preregistered, nested, two-season plus untouched holdout comparisons before it can influence picks.
