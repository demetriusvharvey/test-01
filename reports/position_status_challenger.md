# NFL position-status challenger

The hourly pipeline now freezes observed NFL game-designation counts for nine
position groups and three statuses in the append-only shadow forecast. The
weekly registry can fit a 27-feature ridge logistic adjustment to Elo using
earlier rights-qualified, time-ordered observations. A later prior season
chooses its penalty; future matched game/timestamp forecasts compare log loss
and Brier with frozen Elo. The paired test includes this model in the
availability false-discovery family. The model is never promoted by fitting.

The input covers listed players in both reports and does not estimate an
individual's value, minutes/snaps, a replacement's contribution, confirmed
starters, or the chance a questionable player plays. Practice-only entries
are not counted as game designations. Truncated, conflicting, future or stale
reports are rejected. A missing listing does not mean a player is healthy.
PropLine position counts remain display/research context only; this training
path requires captured nflverse reports and subsequent corroborated finals.

No production coefficient or forward result exists yet. Default training
requires 500 earlier NFL games, 100 validation games and three seasons of
eligible captured reports; the present prospective archive cannot satisfy
that threshold. NBA player-status upstream rights and pregame history are
still unresolved, so NBA remains team Elo. Simulation challengers that lost
to Elo remain excluded. No betting benefit is claimed.

Synthetic integrity tests verify all position groups, freshness, truncation,
time ordering, rights exclusions, and same-time comparison. They are not
validation of predictive accuracy.
