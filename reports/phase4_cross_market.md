# Phase 4: cross-market research

The optional Odds API collector can request `h2h,spreads,totals` together and append separately timestamped, same-book, two-sided spread/total observations to the research branch. It checks source time, event start, market completeness and matching points. It stays **off by default**, requires an existing `ODDS_API_KEY`, and can be explicitly enabled with the GitHub repository variable `ODDS_API_FEATURED_MARKETS=true`; no account or subscription was created.

According to [the provider](https://the-odds-api.com/liveapi/guides/v4/), current odds cost one credit per market per region. For the featured option the pipeline uses at most one call per league per 12 hours, three credits per nonempty call, a 360-credit monthly local cap, and a 60-credit provider reserve. The legacy research-feed job may consume up to about 120 more credits per 30-day month; in 31-day months the theoretical combined usage is at most 484 credits if the local cap applies. Provider-reported remaining credits and the reserve stop this collector earlier. Other applications using the same key can also consume the quota. This schedule is not minute-level coverage.

An empirical residual-distribution challenger conditions on spread and total, preserves discrete NFL score margins/key numbers and rejects rows without pregame market capture and separately observed final timestamps. It defaults to a minimum of 500 games and two seasons and remains shadow-only. A same-book, same-game, time-aligned gap can be logged with a supplied research threshold but is not a betting signal. Tests use synthetic examples, not evidence of predictive value.

No fitted spread-to-win probability, threshold, line movement effect or CLV result has been validated. There are currently no qualified collected spread/total samples and no key. Source rights, delays, settlement convention, sampled liquidity, key-number smoothing, model selection, separate season replications, FDR and untouched holdout remain prerequisites to promotion. Pure model probabilities do not use spreads or totals.
