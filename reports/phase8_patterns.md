# Phase 8: preregistered pattern batch

The H010 batch in [PATTERN_REPORT.md](../PATTERN_REPORT.md) covers the preregistered rest, back-to-back, early travel, altitude, division, wind, quarterback-change and overtime hypotheses. Each requires explicit observed times and complete fields, uses one first eligible forecast per game, and records sample counts, signed win-minus-market residuals, day-block uncertainty, raw/Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted p-values and failures. The full batch is logged in append-only `observations/pattern_tests` records on the research data branch.

At the initial October 28 checkpoint, there were 96 captured forecast snapshots but **zero confirmed outcomes** in the matched forward forecast database, so all ten league/definition checks are `not_testable`. No pattern has a significant result, two-season replication or untouched confirmation, and no pattern enters predictions. The legacy Lakers loss is not a new pure-model forecast and cannot be retroactively added to this study.

Starting with this phase, newly built market snapshots retain each quote's provider and observed time. Earlier forecasts lacking provider provenance do not gain it retroactively. Current NBA historical data rights and AgentBets bulk-archive rights are unresolved; they are not assumed verified merely because data is publicly readable. Additional requested patterns (such as lineup-specific minutes, fatigue, travel itineraries, team/ref interactions and money-flow-based reverse line movement) remain unavailable until relevant information is captured and their exact hypotheses are registered before testing.

An additional pre-registered H013 family now covers blowout wins/losses, short weeks, byes, early-season underdogs, heavy underdogs, wide same-time book disagreement and NFL prime-time favorites. These definitions and H010 are adjusted as one combined family, with every insufficient, failed or negative result retained. They use only fields actually saved in a pregame forecast; absent games-played, prior-margin or source values remain unknown. The expanded batch is not an endorsement of these theories or a promoted model feature.
